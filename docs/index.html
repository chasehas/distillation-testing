Key Scaling Insights

  • 1. The premium is largest when the student has the most to learn: On the smaller 0.5B student, frontier CoT distillation produces an astounding +17.0 percentage point premium over human crowdworker solutions and +37.0 pp over base floor.
  • 2. The premium shrinks as student capability increases: The 1.5B model already possesses strong mathematical reasoning zero-shot (scoring 62.0% on base floor), compressing the distillation premium to +4.0 pp over human SFT.
  • 3. Fine-tuning a capable student on weak data can actually hurt it: On the 1.5B model, fine-tuning on organic crowdworker solutions degraded performance from 62.0% down to 54.0%, whereas GPT-4 frontier distillation preserved high accuracy (58.0%).
