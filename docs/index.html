Key Findings

  • 1. The distillation premium is largest when the student has the most to learn — the 0.5B model gains +17 pp from GPT-4 data vs. human data.
  • 2. The premium shrinks as model capability increases — the 1.5B model already scores 62% untrained, compressing the premium to +4 pp.
  • 3. Training a capable model on weak data can hurt it — the 1.5B model drops from 62% to 54% after training on human solutions, while GPT-4 data preserves 58%.
